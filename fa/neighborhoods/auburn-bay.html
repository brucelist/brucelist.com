About Auburn Bay

Auburn Bay is a southeast Calgary community developed by Brookfield Residential beginning in the mid-2000s. It is anchored by a 43-acre freshwater lake (the largest in any Calgary lake community) with a sandy beach, swimming, fishing, paddleboarding in summer, and skating and ice hockey in winter. Lake access is included with mandatory HOA membership.

The community sits next to the South Health Campus hospital, Seton's expanding retail and dining district (with the YMCA, Cineplex, and public library), and Stoney Trail for quick access across the city. Housing stock includes detached homes, duplexes, townhomes, and condos, making Auburn Bay accessible at multiple price points.

Auburn Bay buyers are typically families and healthcare workers drawn to the lifestyle, schools, and proximity to South Health Campus. Investors should note the strong rental demand around the hospital, but factor HOA fees and condo board rules into their analysis.
