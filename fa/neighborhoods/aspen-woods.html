About Aspen Woods

Aspen Woods sits on the western edge of Calgary, bordered by 17th Avenue SW, Highway 8, and the West Springs and Springbank Hill communities. Developed primarily in the 2000s and 2010s, it features a mix of executive single-family homes, estate properties, townhomes, and a small number of low-rise condos clustered around Aspen Landing — a popular shopping and dining hub.

The community is consistently ranked among Calgary's most desirable for families, thanks in part to nearby private schools (Webber Academy, Calgary Academy, Rundle College) and strong public schools. Commuters appreciate the quick connection to downtown via Bow Trail or 17th Avenue, and weekend warriors love being 10 minutes closer to Banff than most of the city.

Aspen Woods buyers tend to be discerning — they care about builder reputation, lot orientation, and finishing quality. Sellers benefit from skilled marketing that highlights the lifestyle this community offers. I bring that experience to every transaction.
