About Tuscany

Tuscany is a northwest Calgary community developed primarily through the 1990s and 2000s, tucked between Crowchild Trail, Stoney Trail, and the Twelve Mile Coulee escarpment. Residents enjoy direct access to the Tuscany LRT station on the Red Line, the Tuscany Club (a private residents' association with pools, tennis, and skating rinks), and an extensive network of pathways connecting to the Bow River and Bowmont Park.

The housing mix is predominantly detached single-family homes, with pockets of townhomes and estate-style properties along the ravine. Buyers are typically drawn to Tuscany for its family-friendly schools, mountain views, and a 30-minute commute to downtown via the LRT or Crowchild Trail.

Whether you are upsizing into a Tuscany Estates home or looking for an entry point in the original Tuscany subdivisions, knowing the micro-pockets, Tuscany Springs, Tuscany Hills, Tuscany Estates, Tuscany Ravine, makes a real difference. I have sold homes here and know the streets, the builders, and the resale considerations.
