About the Beltline

The Beltline runs immediately south of downtown Calgary, bounded roughly by the CP rail line to the north, Macleod Trail to the east, 17th Avenue to the south, and 14th Street to the west. It encompasses three historic districts, Connaught, Victoria Park, and West Connaught, and is home to the Stampede Grounds, 17th Avenue's "Red Mile," and Central Memorial Park.

Inventory is overwhelmingly high- and mid-rise condos, with a mix of older 1980s concrete buildings, character walk-ups, and newer glass towers. The Beltline appeals to first-time buyers, investors targeting strong rental demand, and anyone who wants to walk to work, +15 access, the C-Train, and a long list of restaurants and patios.

Building selection matters enormously here, condo fees, deferred maintenance, special assessments, and rental restrictions vary widely between towers. I review condo documents carefully with every buyer and have years of experience navigating Beltline transactions on both sides of the deal.
